CHK

Strength checker

Type a password and see what it is really worth: the size of the character set it uses, the randomness that implies, how long an offline attacker needs to reach it, and any pattern that gives it away early. Nothing is stored and nothing is sent anywhere.

Reading the estimate

  • The estimate assumes every character was chosen at random and that an attacker can try 100 billion guesses per second against a stolen hash. Real systems differ, but the shape of the answer holds.
  • Predictable substitutions cost more than they look like they do: p@ssw0rd is a known word with two swaps, and guessing tools try those transformations first.
  • Anything you type here stays in this tab. Even so, the safest habit is to test a password of the same shape rather than one you actually use.
